How net salary is calculated in Greece

1. Social security (EFKA)

The employee pays 13.37% of the gross salary to e-EFKA for pension, health and unemployment insurance. From 1 April 2027 it is planned to drop to 12.87%. The employer pays another 21.79% on top. Both stop at a monthly ceiling of €7,761.94.

2. Income tax

Tax is calculated on the yearly income after social security, with progressive brackets from 9% to 44%. Lower rates apply to people up to 30 and to families with children. Employees then get a tax credit of at least €777, which is reduced for higher incomes.

3. Monthly withholding

Your employer estimates the yearly tax and withholds it equally from each payment. With 14 payments, the bonuses are taxed the same way, so the net Christmas bonus is roughly one net monthly salary.

Moving to Greece: the 50% tax break (Article 5C)

If you transfer your tax residence to Greece and take a job here, half of your employment income can be exempt from Greek income tax for 7 years. You must not have been a Greek tax resident in 5 of the 6 years before the move, and you apply to the tax authority (AADE) after the move. Social security is still paid on the full salary. Frequently asked questions

What is the net salary for €1,500 gross in Greece in 2027?

With the 2027 rules, €1,500 gross a month gives about €1,171 net from April 2027 (€1,163 in January–March), for an employee over 30 with no children and 14 payments. That is about €16,371 net a year. The employer pays about €1,827 a month including its own social security.

How much is the minimum wage in Greece after tax?

The minimum wage is €920 gross a month for full-time work (from 1 April 2026), paid 14 times a year. That is about €776 net a month from April 2027 (€771 in January–March 2027), or about €10,844 net a year. A new increase is expected in April 2027.

How much gross salary do I need for €2,000 net?

About €2,852 gross a month in 2027, with 14 payments, no children and age over 30. Press “Net → Gross” above to get the exact gross for your own situation.

Why are there 14 salaries a year in Greece?

Private-sector employees get a Christmas bonus (one monthly salary, paid by 21 December), an Easter bonus (half a salary) and a holiday allowance (half a salary). With the 12 regular salaries this makes 14 payments. Job offers in Greece usually quote the monthly gross, so multiply by 14 to compare with a yearly salary.

How much does the 50% expat tax break save?

For a €3,000 gross monthly salary in 2027 (14 payments, no children), the yearly net rises from about €29,109 to about €34,646: roughly €5,537 more a year, or €396 more per payment. The exemption lasts for 7 tax years.

How much does an employee cost the employer in Greece?

On top of the gross salary the employer pays 21.79% social security. A €1,000 gross salary costs €1,217.90 a month and €17,050.60 a year with 14 payments.
